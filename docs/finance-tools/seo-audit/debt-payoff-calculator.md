## Gaps found (before)

- No one-off extra payment ("snowflake" in spreadsheet tools; "with extra payments" queries).
- No guidance on managing several cards (due dates, autopay), which the multi-card leader covers.
- "How to Use the Calculator" still at position 2 (templated feel); examples came late.

## What changed

- **Tool:** one-off extra payment (amount + month) routed to the priority debt; engine `simulatePlan(..., extras)`; new test (three cards + $1,000 in month 4: avalanche 17 months, $1,301.06). Month-by-month plan table and its schedule test kept.
- **Content outline (new):** What Is a Credit Card Debt Payoff Calculator? (usage folded in) → Example: Paying Off Three Credit Cards → Avalanche or Snowball: Which Clears Your Cards Faster? → What a One-Off Payment Does → Example: Cards and Loans Together → Your Payoff Plan in Excel → Ways to Pay Off Credit Card Debt → Managing Several Cards While You Pay Them Down → Using It With Your Card Statements.
- New FAQs: bonus/tax refund in the plan; closing a card after payoff. Benefits updated.
- Words: 1,085 → 1,105 (the structure changed more than the length).
