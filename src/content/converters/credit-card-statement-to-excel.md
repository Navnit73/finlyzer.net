# Convert Credit Card Statements to Excel and CSV

Credit card statements mix purchases, payments, refunds, interest and fees, often with foreign-currency details on a second line and merchant names padded with location codes. Copying them into a spreadsheet by hand is slow, and adding up the wrong column overstates spending. Finlyzers extracts each line into a structured spreadsheet so the numbers are ready to use.

## How Card Statements Differ From Bank Statements

On a bank statement, deposits increase the balance. On a credit card statement it works the other way: **purchases increase what you owe** and **payments reduce it**. The converter keeps charges and credits in separate columns, so a monthly payment is not mistaken for spending and a refund is not mistaken for income.

Card statements also tend to show a **statement summary** (previous balance, payments, purchases, fees, interest and new balance) rather than a running balance on every line. Compare the totals of your Charges and Payments columns with that summary to confirm nothing was missed.

## What the Converter Extracts

- **Transaction dates** for each line.
- **Merchant descriptions**, with wrapped text and foreign-currency notes joined to the right transaction.
- **Charges** and **payments or credits** in separate columns.
- **Reference numbers** where the statement prints them.
- **A suggested category** where the merchant makes it clear.
