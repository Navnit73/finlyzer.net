# Convert Bank Statements to OFX for Xero

OFX (Open Financial Exchange) is a standard bank data format that Xero and many other accounting apps import directly. Finlyzers converts PDF statements into OFX so you can bring in transactions your bank feed missed.

## How to Convert and Import into Xero

1. **Upload** the PDF bank statement above.
2. **Review** the verified transactions.
3. **Download the .ofx file.**
4. In Xero, open the bank account, choose **Manage Account**, then **Import a Statement**, and upload the file.

## When OFX Is the Right Choice

- Your bank has no direct feed to Xero.
- You need history from before the feed was connected.
- A client sends PDF statements and you want them reconciled in Xero quickly.

## Compatible Software

Xero, Zoho Books, GnuCash, MoneyMoney, Microsoft Money, and other OFX-compatible tools.
